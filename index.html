How scores work & their limits

Custom tier points use trait frequency: count ÷ collection supply. Thresholds are exclusive. Points are multiplied by your trait weight, then rounded. Special traits count too. Numeric values use exact frequencies from a complete collection scan, never API min/max ranges. Available OpenSea frequency distributions stay the same across Listed, All and Compare.

“Score missing traits / unavailable data” is opt-in. Checked: an absent trait uses its measured absence frequency when available, with your missing multiplier. An unavailable frequency is treated as rare and labelled “Assumed rare”, not 0%. Unchecked: missing or unavailable data earns no points. At the default settings, an unavailable present trait earns 7 points; an assumed absent trait earns round(7 × 1.5) = 11, before any custom weight.

Missing frequencies and exact numeric values may require a full scan. Pair bonuses require a complete collection population; only the three rarest eligible pairs contribute, using their own multiplier. Positive trait weights do not scale pair bonuses; a zero weight disables both that trait and its pairs. Assumed points are excluded from measured coverage and high-score/lower-price flags.

Scores are not OpenRarity, market values, or comparable rarity measurements across collections. OpenSea rank is separate. “High score / lower price” means top-quartile score and lower-half price within the same fetched collection, chain and currency, with full trait coverage and at least three priced items. No exchange rates are assumed.

A wallet address works without an API key. It is sent to Art Blocks’ public data service to retrieve indexed holdings and features; your OpenSea key is never sent there.
